The authority puzzle
is not a ladder.

Unfold the layers. Each answers a different question. No fold grants the next one.

Evidence can strengthen a claim without permitting an action. Permission can exist without proving the claim that motivated it.

01ObservationWhat was captured?+

A trace, not the thing itself.

Preserve

The captured paper or report, raw hash, canonical metadata, provenance, and capture time.

source observationcontent identity

Limit

Changed bytes establish a changed capture—not necessarily a changed scientific claim. A snapshot does not establish truth.

Capture ≠ truth. Preservation ≠ publication permission.
Unfold an example ↗

A report is captured twice with different hashes. The observation history records the difference. Whether the result changed remains a separate interpretive question.

02Human rulingWhat did you judge, and within what scope?+

Authored judgment survives regeneration.

Preserve

A claim target, verdict, reason, evidence binding, scope, and history. Affirmed, rejected, uncertain, and superseded remain distinct.

Limit

A binding establishes traceability. An affirmation records judgment. Neither automatically confers universal verification.

An inspected passage + an affirmation does not equal unlimited verification.
Unfold an example ↗

“Affirmed for the reported evaluation; not established for deployment.” A later ruling may supersede this judgment without erasing it. Stale targets remain visible.

03Bearing · claim laboratoryHow does the claim stand under a declared evidence policy?+

Different ways of knowing.
Not a vote count for truth.

Bearing evaluates the ledger at a read-point, with an active frame configuration and an explicit policy.

Bₜ(c) = Fold_B(L≤ₜ, F_active, π)
implementationformal-semanticsexternal-literatureadversarial-evaluationreplicationnormative-review

Technicolor marks different methods of inquiry—not six interchangeable endorsements. Distinct tags do not by themselves establish independent support.

robust(c) ⇏ true(c)
Contradictions remain visible; they are not silently deducted from a score.
Unfold the proposed policy and independence rules ↗

As described in the workshop: an explicit refute event yields refuted; otherwise two or more active supporting frames yield robust, one yields fragile, and zero yields gone. This threshold is a policy, not an epistemic theorem.

A tested-by relation contributes only when its applicable latest check outcome is held.

  • Multiple files in one component remain one implementation neighborhood.
  • Formal derivations must do more than restate implementation comments.
  • One paper cannot manufacture several independent frames.
  • Adversarial tests use predeclared operators and observable oracles.
  • Replication must not share the tested decision logic.
  • Author-originated judgment remains provenance, not automatically independent normative support.
04Governing policy · explicit bridgeWhat conditions must this action satisfy?+

A gate, not an inference.

A governing policy may make a Bearing result one prerequisite for a particular action. The policy must explicitly establish that dependency. A change in evidentiary standing alone does not grant or revoke capability.

Stratum

The system of record for durable operational decisions and their governing basis.

Stele

Defines enforceable policy. It does not narrate personal meaning or turn research standing into permission by implication.

“The claim is robust” is not an authorization rule.
Unfold a hypothetical bridge ↗

“For this named action, require an existing scoped capability, a valid governing basis, and a robust Bearing result.” This is an illustrative rule only—not a proposed default policy or a live grant.

05Runtime authorityWhat may the agent do, and on what basis?+

Permission has its own type.

A(x) = (g, b)

Here, g denotes governing capability and b the basis under which it was obtained. This is separate from the standing of a research claim.

robust(c) ⇏ authorized(x)
authorized(x) ⇏ true(c)

The interpreter remains accountable for the direction chosen. No evidence fold takes custody of that responsibility.

Counterexample bench / local simulation only

Try to cross the boundary.

Change the inputs. The illustrative policy always requires a scoped capability and valid basis. You can separately choose whether it requires robust evidence.
