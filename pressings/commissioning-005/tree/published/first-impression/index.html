The first impression.

Preparation is not publication.

This is a test piece for the press, not an essay drawn from a private archive. It exists to make the publishing boundary visible.

Three deliberate acts

  1. Prepare. Select a reviewed directory. Compile its routes and local references. Build the surrounding site before sealing anything.
  2. Verify. Check the complete inventory against the recorded bytes. Inspect the result and retain its seal separately.
  3. Publish. Name the pressing and the seal you reviewed. Deploy that exact tree, without rebuilding it.

What remains unchanged

A release label cannot be reused by the tool. Deployment receipts sit outside the pressing. New work requires a new pressing.

A fingerprint proves byte identity against a trusted reference. It does not prove authorship, truth, or the wisdom of publication.
